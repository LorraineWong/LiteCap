# Code of Conduct

LiteCap follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are using a second language — English and Chinese are both welcome.
- Assume good intent, and critique ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or sharing other people's private information.
- Keep screenshots you attach free of personal or confidential data (LiteCap's mosaic tool helps).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules,
and may block people who keep doing so.

To report a problem privately, use the **Report content** option on the comment, or contact the maintainer
through the email address on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/LorraineWong).
Reports are handled confidentially.
